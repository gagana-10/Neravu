FIRST AID INFORMATION

Minor cuts:
For a minor cut, gently clean the wound with clean water and apply gentle
pressure if there is minor bleeding. A clean dressing can be used to
protect the wound.

Seek medical attention if the wound is deep, bleeding does not stop,
there is significant injury, or signs of infection develop.

Minor burns:
For a minor burn, cool the affected area with cool running water.
Do not apply ice directly to the burn. Serious or extensive burns
require professional medical attention.
