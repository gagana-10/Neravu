GENERAL HEALTH INFORMATION

Headache:
Headaches can have many causes, including dehydration, lack of sleep,
stress, or other conditions. Rest, adequate fluids, and monitoring symptoms
may help with mild headaches. Persistent, severe, or unusual headaches
should be evaluated by a healthcare professional.

Fever:
Fever is an increase in body temperature that can occur during infections
and other conditions. Rest and adequate fluid intake are generally important.
A persistent, very high, or concerning fever should be evaluated by a
healthcare professional.

Dehydration:
Common signs of dehydration can include thirst, dry mouth, dark urine,
tiredness, dizziness, and reduced urination. Drinking appropriate fluids
may help with mild dehydration. Severe symptoms require medical attention.
